Privacy Policy

Map and Motives Uploader · Last updated: October 18, 2026

Map and Motives Uploader is a private tool that the owner of the YouTube channel Map & Motives uses to manage their own channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

YouTube API Services

Map and Motives Uploader uses the YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's Privacy Policy applies to data handled by Google.

What data the tool accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), the tool:

  • uploads the owner's own video files, thumbnails and caption files to the owner's channel
  • sets and reads the titles, descriptions, tags, schedule and settings of the owner's own videos
  • reads the processing and status details of the owner's own videos
  • reads the owner's own channel analytics (for example views, watch time and audience retention) and comments on the owner's own videos, to improve future videos

It does not access other channels or users' private data, and it does not collect personal information from viewers.

How data is stored and shared

  • The sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer.
  • Video files, video details and analytics summaries stay with the channel owner and on YouTube.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • This website uses no cookies or tracking.

Removing access

You can revoke the tool's access at any time from your Google Account security settings. Deleting the token file on the computer also removes access.
