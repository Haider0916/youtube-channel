Map and Motives Uploader

A private tool for the YouTube channel Map & Motives.

Map and Motives Uploader is a small private tool that the owner of the YouTube channel Map & Motives uses to upload and schedule their own educational videos, and to read their own channel's statistics. It runs only for the channel owner, is not offered to the public, and has no other users.
