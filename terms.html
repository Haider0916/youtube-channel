Map and Motives Uploader · Last updated: October 18, 2026

1. What it is

Map and Motives Uploader is a private tool that the owner of the YouTube channel Map & Motives uses to upload and schedule their own videos and to read their own channel's statistics. It is not distributed, sold or offered to anyone else, and has no other users.

2. YouTube Terms of Service

Map and Motives Uploader uses the YouTube API Services. Anyone using it agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data handled by Google is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

3. What it does

  • Uploads the owner's own videos, thumbnails and English caption files to the owner's own channel.
  • Sets each video's title, description, tags, settings and scheduled publish time.
  • Reads the owner's own videos' status, analytics and comments to confirm uploads and improve future videos.

It does not access other channels or users, does not display YouTube content to the public, and is not monetized.

4. Data

See the Privacy Policy. In short: the sign-in token stays on the owner's computer, nothing is shared with third parties, and access can be revoked at any time.

5. Changes

If the tool's use changes, these terms and the Privacy Policy will be updated, and YouTube will be notified as its Developer Policies require.
